Zach is a multidisciplinary designer based in Richmond, VA. He is currently a Studio Monitor & Instructor at Studio Two Three, has recently done Art Direction for The Commonwealth Times, and is actively freelancing.


Zach's practice specializes in editorial design and physical typographic printing. His recent work explores constraints, production, precedents, and translation.
